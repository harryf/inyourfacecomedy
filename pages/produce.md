IN YOUR FACE Comedy runs the comedy at [ROBIN's](https://robins-coffee.ch/) in Zürich: [Comedy Brew](/comedybrew/) every Thursday since 2023, and a growing set of shows produced by other comedians and promoters. If you have a show and an audience of your own, this page says how a night here works and what we need from you. There are no prices on this page. Every show is different, and we agree the terms with you before a date is confirmed.

## The room

ROBIN's is a bar on Zähringerstrasse in the Niederdorf, two minutes from the Central tram stop and five from the main station. The comedy happens in the back room: a stage corner, a PA with wired microphones, a spotlight, and seating for up to 60 in the standard layout. For bigger nights the room can be rearranged for up to about 120 people. That is a different setup with more lead time, and we agree it case by case.

The bar is ROBIN's. Your guests buy their drinks there, and the bar stays open through the show. There is no food, and our [FAQ](/faq/) tells guests where to eat before.

The audience in Zürich for English comedy is international and mixed: people who moved here last month and people who were born here. [What guests say about the shows](/reviews/).

## What a night with us includes

- The date in the ROBIN's comedy calendar, with the room reserved for you from setup to close.
- Stage, sound and light set up and checked before doors, and packed away afterwards.
- Someone from IN YOUR FACE on site for the whole night: sound check, doors, the bar, the clock.
- Your show on inyourfacecomedy.ch: its own page, a place in the [calendar](/calendar/), and the ticket link where our audience and Google look for it.

## Promotion, if you want it

Many producers bring their own audience and only need the room. If you want ours as well, we promote the show on the channels we have built since 2018 (figures from November 2026):

- The mailing list: about 1,800 subscribers, four in ten of whom open each email. Your show goes in the monthly show email and, for bigger nights, in a dedicated email to past guests of similar shows.
- Instagram: about 2,500 followers. Posts and stories for your show, and a place in the Sunday story of the week's shows.
- The Meetup group (about 900 members), Facebook, Telegram, and the Google listing of ROBIN's, where every show appears as an event.

Paid advertising is yours to run. We are happy to tell you what has worked for us.

## What we need from you

One named person owns the show and brings an audience: a comedian or promoter whose own followers will come. We do not book a show without one. Then the timeline below.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Six weeks before the show, at the latest | The date agreed and the terms settled in writing. Pick a free night from the [calendar](/calendar/) first. |
| Four weeks before | Everything on the list below, so the show can be listed and promoted. |
| Two weeks before | A word on how sales are going, so we can decide together whether the night needs a push. |
| On the day | A count of how many tickets you have sold by 13:00, so we know what to prepare for. You and your acts at ROBIN's at the agreed time before doors. The show starts on time. |

The list for four weeks before:

- Flyer artwork in three formats: square (1080 x 1080) for posts, story (1080 x 1920), and landscape (1920 x 1080) for the website, Meetup and Facebook. The IN YOUR FACE logo goes on every one: [download the logo](/assets/img/inyourface.png).
- The show's title, a one-line hook, and a description of 80 to 150 words. The language of the show, if it is not English.
- The lineup: each act's name, a photo and their Instagram handle, and who hosts, so we can put them on our [list of comedians](/comedians/) and help promote the show.
- Start time, doors, running time with the break, and any age guidance.
- A live ticket link. [Eventfrog](https://eventfrog.ch/) is what our audience knows: free to use and the best-known ticket platform in Switzerland. Eventbrite, Fienta or another platform are fine too, anything with a ticket page we can link to. Your own account is the normal case; ticketing through ours is possible by arrangement. Tickets go on sale the day the listing goes live.
- Anything you need beyond the standard setup. A second microphone is no problem. For music, your phone connects to the sound system over Bluetooth, so a Spotify playlist for walk-in and the break is easy. A table for merch, say so.

## How the money works

You sell the tickets and keep the ticket income. There is a fee for the night, which covers the room, the setup and the person on site, and a separate fee if you want us to promote the show. Both are sized to the show: the layout, the day of the week and what you need from us. We agree them in writing before the date is confirmed (a WhatsApp message is fine), together with what happens if the show is cancelled or moved. The bar stays with ROBIN's.

## How we work

- Your show is listed under IN YOUR FACE Comedy, so it carries our name as well as yours. The one thing our guests expect without fail is a show that starts on time. Switzerland runs on punctuality.
- The IN YOUR FACE backdrop hangs behind the stage at every show here. You can see it in [this reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dddz788tx-2/).
- Every new show here starts as a one-off. From there, if you want a regular slot, we can discuss it.
- A QR code on the door invites your guests to join the IN YOUR FACE mailing list, so they hear about the next shows here, yours included.
- Generally we do one comedy show a night, but we are open to discussing it. ROBIN's is shut on Mondays, and Tuesdays and Thursdays are taken by our resident shows. Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays are open, and some Sundays. The [calendar](/calendar/) shows what is booked.
- Recording is welcome. Our [FAQ](/faq/#filming) tells guests they may be filmed. A few photos afterwards for the [gallery](/moments/) are appreciated.
- We say no sometimes. ROBIN's reputation as a comedy room is built one night at a time, and we would rather keep a date free than fill it with a show that is not ready.

## Not been on our stage yet?

Most of the shows that have worked at ROBIN's were started by people we had already seen on stage here. If we have not met, [do a spot at Comedy Brew](/perform/) first, or come to a show and say hello. It is the quickest way for both sides to know this is a fit.

## How to start

Email [harry@inyourfacecomedy.ch](mailto:harry@inyourfacecomedy.ch) with:

- who you are, and links to your channels;
- the show: title, format, language, the acts you have in mind;
- two or three possible dates from the [calendar](/calendar/), at least six weeks ahead;
- the audience you expect and where it comes from;
- the ticket price you have in mind (we are happy to help with pricing from our experience), and whether you want promotion from us.

We answer within a few days. If it looks like a fit, we meet at a show or on a call and settle the terms.
