New in town or just visiting? Jokes, Jokes, Jokes is a live English stand-up night, no Swiss German required.

Some jokes will live, some will die, and the audience decides which. Swiss-based comedians host and build a new lineup for every edition: friends, local comics from Zürich and visitors passing through. It is a curated open mic, so comics sign up in advance and you get a mix of fresh material and proven bits, all performed in English.

The crowd is a relaxed mix of internationals, expats and curious locals, so it's an easy night out whether you come alone or with friends. Grab a drink, take a seat and settle in.

ROBIN's is in the Niederdorf old town, two minutes from the Central tram stop and five minutes from the main station. The show runs every Tuesday: doors 7:30pm, show 8:00pm. Tickets are best bought in advance online, since space is limited. Recommended 16+ for adult themes and language.

Part of IN YOUR FACE Comedy, running English-language live shows in Zürich since 2018.
