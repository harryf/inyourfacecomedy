New in town or just visiting? Jokes, Jokes, Jokes is a live English stand-up night, no Swiss German required, and the audience writes part of it.

On the way in you get a post-it. Write a word, a sentence, a confession or a terrible drawing and drop it in the box. Each comic does their own set, then pulls a post-it out and has to turn whatever you wrote into comedy on the spot. Some jokes will live, some will die, and the crowd decides which. Writing one is optional and nobody gets pulled on stage.

A host and a rotating lineup of comics based in Switzerland and passing through, all in English: polished bits, brand-new material and jokes that did not exist until the box opened. The crowd is a relaxed mix of internationals, expats and curious locals, so it's an easy night out whether you come alone or with friends.

ROBIN's is in the Niederdorf old town, two minutes from the Central tram stop and five minutes from the main station. The show runs every Tuesday: doors 7:30pm, show 8:00pm. Tickets are best bought in advance online, since space is limited. Recommended 16+ for adult themes and language.

Part of IN YOUR FACE Comedy, running English-language live shows in Zürich since 2018.
