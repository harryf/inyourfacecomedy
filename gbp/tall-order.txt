TITLE: Tall Order: Prak and Miguel on stage at ROBIN's

New in town or just visiting? Tall Order is a live stand-up open mic performed in English, so you don't need a word of Swiss German to enjoy it.

Prak and Miguel host and open the night, then hand the mic to a lineup of local comics and visiting guests working on new material. It is an evening where new comedians and experienced ones try out fresh jokes. Bring a friend, order a drink and look up.

ROBIN's is in the Niederdorf old town, two minutes from the Central tram stop and five minutes from the main station. The show takes place every second Tuesday at 20:00. Tickets are best bought in advance online.

Part of IN YOUR FACE Comedy, running English-language live shows in Zürich since 2018.
