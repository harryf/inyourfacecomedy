TITLE: Gratis Comedy Zum Mitnehmen - Free Entry Stand Up Comedy

New in town or just visiting? Gratis Comedy Zum Mitnehmen is a live stand-up comedy night where local Swiss comedian Teddy Hall is joined by some of his funniest friends to try out their newest and stupidest new joke ideas.

This is comedy in its rawest and most unpolished form. Expect some things to be hilarious and many to be terrible, but that is the fun of it all. The show features a zero douchebags policy.

This show is free entry and your presence is a present. Donations are accepted at the end of the show.

The event takes place at ROBIN's in the Niederdorf old town, two minutes from the Central tram stop and five minutes from the main station. The show runs from 20:00 to 22:00. Tickets are free entry, donations welcome at the end of the show, and best bought in advance online.

Part of IN YOUR FACE Comedy, running English-language live shows in Zürich since 2018.
