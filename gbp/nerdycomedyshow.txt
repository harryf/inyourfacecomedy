TITLE: The NERDY COMEDY Show, English stand-up in Zürich

New in town or just visiting? The NERDY COMEDY Show is live English-language stand-up in Zürich, so you don't need a word of Swiss German to enjoy it. This is a one-off evening, not a weekly slot.

You know exactly who you are. You've min-maxed a character build. You've explained the multiverse to someone who did not ask. This show is for you.

Some of Switzerland's best English-language comedians bring their sharpest material, then argue with the audience about the stuff that actually matters: which edition of the tabletop dragon game peaked, the worst anime filler arc of all time, and why your unplayed game library is a cry for help.

No system mastery needed. Just bring your hottest nerd take and the willingness to defend it in front of strangers. It's a friendly, international crowd, which makes it an easy night out if you're travelling solo. Grab a drink, find a seat and settle in.

The venue is ROBIN's, a small comedy room in the Niederdorf old town, two minutes from the Central tram stop. Doors open at 7pm and the show starts at 8pm. Seating is limited, so tickets are best bought in advance online, though you can also try your luck at the door. If money is tight right now, get in touch through the website: a few seats are held back, because everyone deserves a laugh.

Part of IN YOUR FACE Comedy, running English-language live shows in Zürich since 2018.
