TITLE: Promessi Spassi: Italian stand-up comedy in Zürich

Visiting Zürich or new in town and missing a laugh in Italian? Promessi Spassi is a live stand-up comedy night performed entirely in Italian, so you don't need a word of Swiss German, or English, to enjoy it.

Hosted by Miguel and Nik, it's an open mic format: experienced comedians road-testing new material alongside emerging talent doing their first sets. The bill mixes comedians from Italy and Ticino, so every night is different and you get to discover new voices in Italian comedy in a relaxed, informal room.

It's an easy evening if you're travelling solo, here for work or simply want to meet other Italian speakers in the city. Grab a drink, take a seat and settle in. The crowd is friendly and the front row is safe.

The show runs at ROBIN's in the Niederdorf old town, two minutes from the Central tram stop. Show starts at 8pm and wraps up around 10pm, in plenty of time for the last tram. Tickets are CHF 10, best bought in advance online.

Please note that comedians may record their sets, so you might appear in a clip. If you'd rather not, just let us know and we'll take it down.

Spassi... promessi!

Part of IN YOUR FACE Comedy, running English-language live shows in Zürich since 2018.
