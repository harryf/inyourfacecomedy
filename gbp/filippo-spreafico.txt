TITLE: Filippo Spreafico • Italian Rhapsody

New in town or just visiting? Filippo Spreafico brings his solo show Italian Rhapsody to ROBIN's for one night only. The show is performed in English, so you don't need a word of Swiss German to enjoy it.

Filippo has performed on Comedy Central Italy and RAI, and tours across Europe and the UK in both English and Italian. His show features an hour of stand-up with sharp observations, absurd storytelling, live music, and a healthy dose of chaos. Expect stories about failed relationships, life abroad, and pigeons. Corriere della Sera called him surreal, effective, and his peak of power.

ROBIN's is in the Niederdorf old town, near the Central tram stop. Doors open at 19:30 and the show runs from 20:00 to 22:00. Tickets are CHF 20 early bird or CHF 25 after, and are best bought in advance online.

Part of IN YOUR FACE Comedy, running English-language live shows in Zürich since 2018.
