An English stand-up open mic hosted by Prak and Miguel, who between them have more height than most lineups. New comedians and experienced ones try out fresh jokes at ROBIN's every second Tuesday, alternating with [Jokes, Jokes, Jokes](/jokesjokesjokes/).

<!--more-->

Get ready to scroll. A lot.

![Tall Order: Prak and Miguel on stage at ROBIN's](/assets/img/uploads/tall-order_story.png "Tall Order: English Stand-up Comedy Open Mic")

Well done. You made it.

Prak ( [@returnoftheprak](https://instagram.com/returnoftheprak) ) and Miguel ( [@miguelvitaliicomedy](https://instagram.com/miguelvitaliicomedy) ) host and open the night, then hand the mic to a lineup of local comics and visiting guests working on new material. Bring a friend, order a drink and look up.

## When and Where

- **When:** every second Tuesday, show at 20:00
- **Where:** ROBIN's, Zähringerstrasse 33, 8001 Zürich
- **Tickets:** on Eventfrog, use the button above

## Getting there

ROBIN's is on <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/aac8sjwkZjsTQaAT8">Zähringerstrasse 33</a>, two minutes from the Central tram stop and five from the main station. It's a café and bar by day that turns into a room for comedy and live music at night.
