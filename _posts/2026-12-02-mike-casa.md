## 2 DEC: Mike Casa Stand-up Comedy Show @ROBIN'S ZURICH!

For the **first time ever**, Mike Casa is bringing his hit **ENGLISH** stand-up comedy show to **Robin's Coffee** in the **heart of Zurich**! After performing to more than 14,000+ spectators in 2025, he is back and ready to deliver the laughs at a brand-new venue.

![Mike Casa Show Zurich flyer: Mike Casa smiling with a microphone in front of a packed circus tent audience, with the words Mike Casa Show ZURICH! @ROBIN's coffee](/assets/img/uploads/mike-casa_feature.jpeg "Mike Casa Stand-up Comedy Show at ROBIN's Zürich, Wednesday 2 December 2026")

With over **400M+ views and 20M+ likes** across TikTok and Instagram, Mike Casa isn't quite viral... but he’s at least bacterial. He will serve up a brilliant mix of **brand-new material** and fan-favorite crowd-pleasers. Accompanied by a stellar lineup of guest comedians as opening acts, you are in for **2 hours of pure, unadulterated entertainment!**
