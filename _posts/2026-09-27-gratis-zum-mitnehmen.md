## Gratis Comedy Zum Mitnehmen - Free Entry Stand Up Comedy

It costs money to breathe in Zürich, but it shouldn't cost any to laugh. Join us at this laid-back new material night where local Swiss comedian Teddy Hall is joined by some of his funniest friends to try out their newest and stupidest new joke ideas.

![Gratis Comedy Zum Mitnehmen - Free Entry Stand Up Comedy at ROBIN's](/assets/img/uploads/gratis-zum-mitnehmen_feature.png "Gratis Comedy Zum Mitnehmen - Free Entry Stand Up Comedy")

This is comedy in its rawest and most unpolished form: expect some things to be hilarious and many to be teeeeerrible. But that's the fun of it all! We do not believe in charging audiences for comedy that is not yet developed. Beyond this, this is the only comedy night in Zürich with a zero douchebags policy, which in Zürich is pretty impressive (admittedly the person writing this is from Basel).

This show is free entry and your presence is a present. Donations are accepted at the end of the show, and if you're gainfully employed and want to support the arts, please do. If you're broke? Just come and laugh. Have a look at [@teddyhallcomedy](https://www.instagram.com/teddyhallcomedy) and [@inyourfacecomedy](https://instagram.com/inyourfacecomedy) to see what you're in for.
